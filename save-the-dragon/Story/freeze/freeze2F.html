Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Bol, Micah, and Ray.

[Return regretful.]

You stare at the dragon's still body.

It's already dead. Even if you have a change of heart now, you can't change it. Besides, it was a dragon! With this one being so close to the capital, it was only a matter of time until it got ideas about taking the princess for itself. Best to kill it now and be done with the whole thing.

...But it doesn't feel very knightly, does it? To kill something while it's trapped and unable to defend itself? Not that a dragon could ever be considered defenseless. Then again, it hadn't even tried to use its fire until right at the end.

But it's over now. You might as well start the trek back to the capital.

You turn around to leave. But the ground is slick with the dragon's blood and you slip backwards, your head hitting a rock with a loud crack. The world goes black.

Try again?
