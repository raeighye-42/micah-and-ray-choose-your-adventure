Save the Dragon

A choose-your-own adventure by Micah, Bol, and Ray.

You are a knight.
You find a dragon.

The dragon is trapped. It's tangled in a mess of chains. The chans are thin, but strong, digging deeper into its flesh the more it struggles to free itself. You grip the hilt of the sword at your hip, ready to draw it at a moments notice.

Before you can, the dragon spots you. Its movements stop, and it looks at you with wide eyes. You need to act now, before it has the chance to call on the fire in its belly and fry you where you stand.

What do you do?

Slay the dragon. Stop, unsure. Free the dragon.
